Supervisor approval on a phone

GateKeeper · Part B3. Designed for Joel at 2 AM: one hand, tired eyes, a request on the chlorine system. Dark by default so the screen doesn’t dazzle a night-shift supervisor; text is high-contrast and nothing relies on colour alone.

Why it looks like this

  • What and how risky, before who and why. Asset ID and a risk label (text plus a warning symbol, not just red) lead the screen, because that decides how carefully the supervisor reads the rest.
  • Friction matches risk. Medium-risk assets use a normal Approve button. High-risk assets need a hold-to-approve. Reject is always one tap.
  • Honest about the rules. “Approving late does not extend the window” and “you can’t give both approvals” surface the backend rules (R2, R3) before the supervisor hits an error.
  • One state, one message. Each state opens with a plain-language headline and a single next step, and shows no approve action when none is possible.
  • Built for the setting. 56 px touch targets, primary actions in the thumb zone, dark theme, Atkinson Hyperlegible for look-alike characters (0/O, 1/l), and an icon plus words for every state.
  • Not shown yet. Push-notification entry point, the revoke flow for an active grant, and the medium-risk single-approval variant of screen 1.
